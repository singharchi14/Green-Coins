Spending

How coins are spent

Planned · not final
  • Minimum balance to redeem, for example 20 coins.
  • Each redemption code works once and expires after a short time.
  • Coins can cover only part of a bill.
  • Coins expire after 6 months.
  • Coins are loyalty points. They have no cash value and can’t be transferred or withdrawn.
Fair use and privacy

One household, one account

  • Repeated fake photos lead to suspension.
  • Approved and rejected photos aren’t stored. A photo waiting for review is deleted once a reviewer decides.
  • Your mobile number is used only for sign-in and program updates.

How it works

Sort your waste. Earn Green Coins. Spend them in your mohalla.

Five steps, from sign-up to the shop counter. Scroll to follow them.

Step 1 of 5

Register

Sign up with your mobile number. We verify it with a one-time code.

Step 2 of 5

Sort and photograph

Keep your waste separated into wet, dry, recyclable, hazardous and e-waste. Take one clear photo per category.

Step 3 of 5

Verified by AI

Our AI checks that the photo matches the category you chose. If it is unsure, a person reviews it, usually within a day.

Step 4 of 5

Earn coins

Approved photos add Green Coins to your wallet straight away.

Step 5 of 5

Spend locally

Show your redemption code at a partner shop to get the offer.

Photo tips

Before you tap the camera

  • Take it fresh, in good light, with the waste clearly in frame.
  • Don’t use screenshots or internet images.
  • Don’t use photos with people’s faces.

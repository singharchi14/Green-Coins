Partner shops

Bring neighbours to your shop. Accept Green Coins.

Who can join

Local shops in the pilot mohallas

Kirana stores, tea stalls, repair shops and pharmacies.

What you get

Footfall, and a listing

More footfall from households that are actively taking part, and a listing as a Green Coins partner.

How it works

You choose the offer

A free tea or ₹20 off, for example. The citizen shows a code, you verify it, and the offer is applied.

Your controls

Your limits

  • A daily limit on redemptions.
  • You can pause anytime.

Rules for shops: no raising prices for coin users, and no redeeming at your own shop.

How to apply

What we need from you

  • Shop name
  • Owner’s name
  • Mobile number
  • Address
  • Licence or registration number

This is followed by approval.
